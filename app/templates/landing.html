01 / How it works

From dataset to reality check.

Before the training run. Before the leaderboard drop. Before a broken model reaches production.

  1. 01

    Upload or run the CLI

    Start with train.csv, add an optional test.csv, and name your target column.

    Leakage, found.

    Single-feature AUC, deterministic functions of the target, post-outcome columns like paid_date. Leakproof names the column, shows the evidence, and tells you to drop it.

  2. 02

    7 checks in under a minute

    Get a 0–100 score, a grade from A to F, and findings ranked by how much they matter.

    Clean splits, clean labels.

    Exact and near-duplicate rows across train and test, duplicates inside train, and the rows whose labels are probably wrong, listed by index so you can fix them today.

  3. 03

    Fix plan with code

    Claude turns the findings into a plain-English narrative and a fix plan with code you can use.

    Honest cross-validation.

    It discovers group and time structure in your data, reruns your CV the right way, and shows the gap: "random KFold overstates by 0.11 AUC; use GroupKFold on user_id." Code included.

02 / What we catch

Six ways your dataset lies.

Every finding comes with evidence and a way forward. Here are a few of the fixes.

CHECK / 01

Target leakage

Find the features that reveal the answer, including columns that only exist after the outcome.

X = X.drop(columns=["cancellation_date"])
CHECK / 02

Train/test contamination

Surface exact and near-duplicate rows across train and test before they turn your holdout into a rehearsal.

# Indices flagged by the audit
train = train.drop(index=overlap_indices)
CHECK / 03

Duplicates

Catch repeated rows inside your training data that can land in different folds and inflate your validation score.

# Remove before creating folds
train = train.drop_duplicates()
CHECK / 04

Label noise

Get the row indices of likely wrong labels so you can review the evidence and correct the source.

# Review before changing labels
train.loc[suspect_indices].to_csv("review.csv")
CHECK / 05

Distribution shift

Use adversarial validation to find train/test shift and identify where representative data or reweighting is needed.

# After reviewing covariate shift
model.fit(X, y, sample_weight=shift_weights)
CHECK / 06

Wrong cross-validation

Discover group or time structure, choose the right split, and see an honest CV estimate alongside the inflated score.

from sklearn.model_selection import GroupKFold
cv = GroupKFold(5).split(X, y, groups=customer_id)

Illustrative fixes above. Your report names your columns, rows, and next steps.

04 / Check the code too

Clean data.
Leaky code.

A scaler fitted on train + test, missing values filled with test means, a vocabulary built from every row. leakproof lint follows each value from train.csv and test.csv through your script or notebook and tells you which fit sees test rows, on which line, and how to fix it. Nothing runs and nothing is uploaded.

  • Scripts and Jupyter notebooks (cell + line)
  • Exit code 1 in CI when a fit sees test rows

06 / A few good questions

Before you run it.

The details behind the reality check.

Get in touch
What does the Leakproof Score measure?

The 0–100 score and A–F grade summarize the dataset issues found by the audit. A low score signals problems to investigate before training. The ranked findings explain the evidence, affected columns or rows, and suggested fixes behind the score.

Do I need a test file or a trained model?

No. Start with a tabular train.csv and the name of your target column. Add an optional test.csv to check train/test contamination and distribution shift. You can run the audit before training your model.

Does my data leave my machine?

The free local CLI runs on your machine without uploading your dataset. Hosted audits use the files you upload. Enterprise lets you deploy Leakproof with on-prem Docker in your VPC, where your data stays inside your environment.

What does the AI narrative add?

Claude explains the audit findings in plain English and writes a fix plan with code, helping you understand what to tackle first and why. Pro includes the AI narrative, shareable report links, and PDF export.

Can I use Leakproof in CI or through an API?

Yes. Team includes API access and the CI gate. Use leakproof gate train.csv --target y --min-score 80 to fail the build when a dataset scores below your threshold, or integrate audits through the REST API.

What is the Model Validation Evidence export?

It's an Enterprise export for model-risk and compliance teams that need dataset audit evidence for their review process, including SR 11-7 and EU AI Act workflows. It supports your team's validation review; it does not certify a model's compliance.
